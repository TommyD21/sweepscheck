⚖️ What is SweepsCheck?

SweepsCheck is a reference directory — not a gambling service, casino, or legal advisor. It does not operate sweepstakes, accept entries, or award prizes. Its sole purpose is to help users understand the legal landscape for sweepstakes casinos in their state, using evidence sourced from official statutes, regulatory actions, and operator Terms of Service.

📋 Data sources and methodology

Every legal claim is traced to a primary source in this priority order: (1) Official state statutes and legislative records, (2) State regulatory agencies and AG offices, (3) Official operator Terms of Service and Sweepstakes Rules, (4) Official operator support pages, (5) Reputable secondary sources only when primary sources are unavailable. Secondary sources are clearly labeled. Uncertain information is flagged as uncertain rather than presented as fact. Source attribution is visible in every expanded platform card.

🔄 How data is kept current

A biweekly automated checker runs every 14 days. It monitors key authoritative sources for content changes and staleness. Records older than 30 days are flagged as aging; over 45 days as stale. Any detected legal status changes are flagged for human review and never automatically applied. Legal data is only updated after manual verification against primary sources.

🔒 Privacy

State selections are not stored. All lookups are stateless. This tool does not collect personal data, set cookies for tracking, or share information with advertising partners. It loads fonts from Google Fonts — the only external resource.

⚠️ Limitations and risks

The sweepstakes casino legal landscape is changing rapidly. At least 14 states have enacted bans as of August 2026. New legislation can take effect with little warning. This tool reflects known law as of its last audit date. Always verify the operator's current Terms of Service and your state's law before registering or depositing. This is not a substitute for legal counsel.
